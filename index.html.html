Work

Three original concept projects made by JA.NINJA to show how we think and build. They are concepts, not client work.

Concept project

Tidewater

A booking website concept for a fictional surf school.

Challenge
Make booking a lesson possible in under a minute on a phone.
Approach
One-thumb layout, a clear schedule view and a three-step booking form.
Skills shown
UI design, responsive build, form design
Concept project

Ledger Lane

A brand identity concept for a fictional neighbourhood bookshop.

Challenge
Give a small shop an identity that works on signage and on screen.
Approach
A bold wordmark, a restrained palette and a flexible pattern drawn from book spines.
Skills shown
Identity design, typography, guidelines
Concept project

Northbound

A dashboard interface concept for a fictional freight tracker.

Challenge
Show a lot of shipment data without overwhelming the reader.
Approach
A clear hierarchy, status colours paired with text labels, and filters that stay out of the way.
Skills shown
Product UI, data display, accessibility
